Welcome to Karnataka

About Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India. It is famous for its historical monuments, wildlife, waterfalls, beaches, hill stations, traditional culture and delicious food.


From the ancient ruins of Hampi to the beaches of Gokarna and the royal Mysore Palace, Karnataka offers many interesting places to explore.

Famous Tourist Places

Hampi

Hampi is famous for its ancient temples, monuments and ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Mysore

Mysore is famous for the magnificent Mysore Palace, Dasara celebrations and rich cultural heritage.

Gokarna

Gokarna is a beautiful coastal destination known for its beaches and temples.

Jog Falls

Jog Falls is one of the most famous waterfalls in Karnataka and is surrounded by beautiful greenery.

Historical Places

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage area containing remarkable ruins and temples.

Mysore Palace

A famous royal palace and one of Karnataka's most visited landmarks.

Vijayanagara Heritage

Explore the magnificent architecture and monuments of the Vijayanagara period.

Mysuru Heritage

Mysuru is known for its royal history, architecture and cultural traditions.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

A spectacular waterfall surrounded by forests and hills.

Jog Falls View

The waterfall provides beautiful views, especially during the monsoon season.

Gokarna

A peaceful coastal destination with scenic beaches and natural beauty.

Gokarna Beach

A popular destination for enjoying the Arabian Sea and coastal scenery.

Famous Karnataka Foods

🥞 Mysore Masala Dosa

A crispy dosa served with spicy chutney and potato filling.

🍛 Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional rice dish prepared with lentils, vegetables and spices.

🍬 Mysore Pak

A famous Karnataka sweet made mainly from gram flour, ghee and sugar.

🥥 Neer Dosa

A soft and thin rice-based dosa popular in coastal Karnataka.

🥨 Maddur Vada

A crispy snack that originated in Maddur, Karnataka.

🍞 Mangaluru Buns

Soft and slightly sweet fried bread popular in coastal Karnataka.

Travel Information

  • Best Time to Visit: October to February is generally comfortable for travelling around Karnataka.
  • Transportation: Karnataka has airports, railway stations, buses and taxis connecting major destinations.
  • Popular Destinations: Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hampi, Coorg, Gokarna, Chikkamagaluru and Jog Falls.
  • What to Carry: Comfortable clothes, water, sunscreen, comfortable footwear and a first-aid kit.
  • Food: Try local dishes such as Mysore Masala Dosa, Bisi Bele Bath, Mysore Pak and Neer Dosa.
